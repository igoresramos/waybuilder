## Lore com escolha -- o schema nao tem forma para isso

- `wb:background/animal-whisperer-legacy`: Lore related to one terrain inhabited by animals you like (such as Plains Lore or Swamp Lore).
- `wb:background/bandit-legacy`: Lore related to the terrain you worked in (such as Desert Lore or Plains Lore).
- `wb:background/cultist-legacy`: Lore related to your deity or cult.
- `wb:background/driver-background-451`: Driving Lore or Piloting Lore
- `wb:background/guard-legacy`: Legal Lore or Warfare Lore skill.
- `wb:background/hermit-legacy`: Lore related to the terrain you lived in as a hermit (such as Cave Lore or Desert Lore).
- `wb:background/junk-collector-legacy`: Engineering Lore or Mining Lore skill
- `wb:background/noble-legacy`: Genealogy Lore or Heraldry Lore skill.
- `wb:background/nomad-legacy`: Lore related to one terrain you traveled in (such as Desert Lore or Swamp Lore).
- `wb:background/scout-legacy`: Lore related to one terrain you scouted in (such as Forest Lore or Cavern Lore).
- `wb:background/squire-legacy`: Heraldry Lore or Warfare Lore.
- `wb:background/street-urchin-legacy`: Lore for the city you lived in as a street urchin (such as Absalom Lore or Magnimar Lore).
